# Clinical Encounter Workflow

## Overview

This phase demonstrates the clinical workflow completed during a new patient office visit within Practice Fusion.

The encounter followed a standard primary care workflow, including patient assessment, vital signs documentation, SOAP note creation, diagnosis documentation, preventive care planning, and encounter completion.

---

## 1. Vital Signs Documentation

During the encounter, the patient's vital signs were recorded and stored within the Practice Fusion flowsheet.

The documented observations included:

- Height
- Weight
- Body Mass Index (BMI)
- Blood Pressure
- Temperature
- Pulse
- Respiratory Rate
- Oxygen Saturation
- Pain Score

These structured observations become part of the patient's longitudinal health record and support clinical documentation during the visit.

## 2. Clinical Encounter Documentation

Following collection of vital signs, the patient encounter was documented using the SOAP note workflow.

The encounter included:

- Chief Complaint
- Subjective findings
- Objective findings
- Assessment
- Diagnosis documentation
- Preventive care planning

The assessment documented a routine adult preventive examination without abnormal findings.

## 3. Completed SOAP Documentation

The SOAP note brought together the patient's subjective history, objective findings, clinical assessment, and documented diagnosis within the encounter record.

The encounter documentation included:

**Subjective**
- Reason for visit
- Current health status
- Relevant patient-reported information

**Objective**
- Clinical observations
- Physical assessment findings
- Recorded vital signs

**Assessment**
- Routine preventive examination
- ICD-10-CM diagnosis documentation

**Plan**
- Preventive health guidance
- Continued routine primary care
- Follow-up recommendations

## 4. Diagnosis Documentation

The encounter included the diagnosis:

**Z00.00 — Encounter for general adult medical examination without abnormal findings**

The diagnosis was attached to the encounter and incorporated into the patient's clinical record.

This demonstrates the connection between encounter documentation and structured diagnosis management within Practice Fusion.

---

## Workflow Result

The completed clinical workflow followed:

**Patient Check-In → Office Visit → Vital Signs → SOAP Documentation → Assessment → Diagnosis → Preventive Care Plan**

This demonstrates how Practice Fusion supports structured outpatient clinical documentation within a centralized electronic health record.
