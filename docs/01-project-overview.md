# Practice Fusion EHR Implementation
## CarePoint Family Medical Clinic

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the configuration and use of Practice Fusion for a primary care outpatient workflow at CarePoint Family Medical Clinic.

The implementation focuses on the core patient journey within an electronic health record (EHR), beginning with patient registration and appointment scheduling and continuing through patient check-in, clinical documentation, assessment, care planning, and encounter completion.

The project was designed to demonstrate practical familiarity with navigating and documenting a structured outpatient workflow in Practice Fusion.

---

## Practice Environment

**Platform:** Practice Fusion EHR  
**Practice:** CarePoint Family Medical Clinic  
**Specialty:** Primary Care / Family Medicine  
**Workflow Type:** Outpatient Clinical Care  

All patient information used in this portfolio project is fictional and was created solely for demonstration and training purposes.

---

## Implementation Scope

The Practice Fusion workflow included:

- Patient demographic registration
- Patient chart creation
- Appointment scheduling
- New patient visit setup
- Patient arrival/check-in workflow
- Vital signs documentation
- Medication reconciliation
- Medication allergy reconciliation
- SOAP note documentation
- Clinical assessment
- ICD-10-CM diagnosis documentation
- Preventive care planning
- Encounter review and signing

---

## Workflow

The implemented workflow follows the patient journey:

**Patient Registration → Appointment Scheduling → Check-In → Clinical Encounter → Vitals → Clinical Documentation → Assessment → Care Plan → Signed Encounter**

This structure demonstrates how administrative and clinical information can move through a single EHR workflow while maintaining a centralized patient record.

---

## Key Configuration Areas

### Patient Management

A fictional patient profile was created and configured with demographic and communication information, allowing the patient record to serve as the foundation for subsequent scheduling and clinical activities.

### Appointment Management

A new patient visit was scheduled within the Practice Fusion calendar and progressed through the patient arrival workflow before clinical documentation began.

### Clinical Documentation

The encounter included structured vital signs and SOAP documentation covering subjective findings, objective findings, assessment, and plan.

### Clinical Reconciliation

Medication and medication allergy information were reviewed and documented as part of the encounter workflow.

### Diagnosis and Care Planning

The encounter included an appropriate preventive-care diagnosis and documented recommendations for continued preventive care and follow-up.

### Encounter Completion

Following documentation and review, the encounter was signed and reflected within the patient's longitudinal chart.
